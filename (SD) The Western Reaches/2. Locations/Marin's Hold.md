The 's 300 fisherfolk live in a motte-and-bailey hold where the river meets the bog. A palisade surrounds the village, and Reeve **Tarley Winters** resides in the keep atop a hill with 30 guards. The village is still recovering from an attack by six skrell demons who breached the palisade and slew 20 souls.
Winters believes sorcery attracts the demons, and he recentlyhad three women burned alive on Boot Hill (1205) for suspected witchcraft. Villagers eye each
other warily, and the gossips at The Crayfish Tavern wonder who will be accused next.
## Punishable by Death
After the demon attack, [[Lord Reeve Tarley Winters]] believes that sorcery attracts demons. Three women were recently burned alive for suspected witchcraft. Anything that remotely resembles magic could illicit the harshest of punishments.

## Districts
### 1. Crow's Barrow
The poorest part of the city, leaving outside walls close to the river. Marin's Hold is the only safe point of civilizations for miles around, and more and more people seeks for it's safety. It is better then live by the luck on the wilds
#### a. Gambling Den
The sounds of dice rolling fill the air in the dark den, lit only by candlelight. While the wilds are notably filled with monsters and bandits, plenty of both can be found in here as well.
#### b. Bloodletter
Healing magic, like all magic is strictly forbidden in Marin's Hold, which has allowed the chiurgeon, [[Tarin Denan]], to thrive as a bloodletter. Wether it's fever or the genital pox, bleeding out the sickness is the only cure now...

#### c. The Crayfish Tavern
A haunt for traveling merchants and those passing through the Gloaming. It's not luxurious, but it's the best tavern The Gloaming has to offer.

#### d. Skin of their Teeth
[[Malchor Iriol]] is a trapper who peddles the skins of nearby animals. In addition to skins, he sells [Basic Gear.](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/the-western-reaches-joshywa/a/common-wares-article)
#### e. Bottom's Up Potions and Remedies
[[Kiara Cyrg]]'s shop exists almost in protest to the strict laws against magic. She argues that she's a simple businesswoman, mixing plants and providing cures, but fewer and fewer people dare visit her shop.

Her potions cost 1d4x100gp, and there are NO refunds.
However, she can sell a **healing potion** for 200gp.
#### f. Wares for a Pence
This shop is run by [[Regina Pence]] and her wife, [[Mariel Pence]]. They sell basic wares. Occasionally, Regina has rare items that she will part with.

## Description
```
Lord Reeve is the last of his faction, [[Lords of the woods]] . Once there were six of them. Each with great castles built as a tribute to their power. One by one, the lords fell, their castles destroyed not long after their death. The previous lord taught him that Marin's Hold only stood because they were strong and humble, though in truth it is because there were austere and superstitious. Lord Reeve has always been an unforgiving lord, and each day he grows colder and crueler.
```
## History
The child of founders of the Knight of St. Ydris, he idolized his mother. His first spouse was a Ruler, they had one son, but his spouse won total custody. Without his spouse to temper him he grew out of control.
